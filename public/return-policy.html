Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Shipping Policy Return & Refund Policy

1. Quality Commitment & Guarantee

At Hi Suvai Traditional Foods (hisuvai.com), customer satisfaction and food safety are our highest priorities. All our healthy health mixes, porridge powders, and traditional flours are prepared with 100% natural, unadulterated ingredients under strict hygienic standards.

2. Eligibility for Return or Replacement

Due to the perishable nature of food products, returns are accepted under the following conditions within 7 days of delivery:

  • Package delivered was physically damaged or tampered with during transit.
  • Wrong product or variant was delivered.
  • Product seal was broken upon initial unboxing.

3. How to Request a Return or Replacement

To initiate a return or replacement for your nutritious food products, please follow these steps:

  1. Take a clear photo or unboxing video showing the damaged package or wrong product received.
  2. Contact our customer support team on WhatsApp at 63819 26567 or email hiisuvai@gmail.com with your Order ID.
  3. Our team will verify the details within 24 hours and arrange a free replacement or full refund.

4. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved:

  • For online payments (UPI / Net Banking), refunds will be credited back to your original payment account within 3 to 5 business days.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds will be transferred via Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm / Bank Transfer as per your preference.

5. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to dispatch by contacting us on WhatsApp (63819 26567). Once an order has been dispatched from our Tirunelveli unit, cancellation is no longer possible.

We are always ready to help! Contact us for any issues:
